# noren art direction

Generated with ChatGPT's image model (via the Codex image tool on a ChatGPT plan). Source PNGs are kept out of git; the app ships WebP copies.

- **Style and palette:** warm modern editorial illustration with soft grain, flat shapes and gentle gradients, palette indigo noren curtain #24305e, warm cream #f6efe2, persimmon #f26b3a, sage #8fb39a, gold #d9a441.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, readable text, real people, other designers' artwork.
- **Files:** `packages/web/public/art/wif/`
