Now the customer's side: the Noren app. Sign in with Privy, or connect a browser wallet. Home shows every balance, and you can paste a link and pay privately, right here. Bring USDC to Arc from any chain: Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or Robinhood. Circle burns it on one side and attests, and Noren's hook mints it on Arc. These ran live on public testnets, from the app.
